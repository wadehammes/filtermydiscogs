Closing **`ReleaseModal`** does not stop playback. **Play in background** or a matched track row click calls **`startPlayback`**, replaces the queue with playable tracks from that album starting at the clicked row, and overwrites whatever is in the dock. Unmatched embeddable videos (deduped by YouTube id) render in **`ReleasePlaybackPreview`** as track-style rows without a position column; row click calls **`startReleasePreview`**, and **Add to queue** calls **`addPreviewToQueue`**. **`findVideoForTrack`** ([`releasePlayback.ts`](../../src/utils/releasePlayback.ts); matching in [`releasePlaybackTrackVideoMatching.ts`](../../src/utils/releasePlaybackTrackVideoMatching.ts)) matches on normalized substring overlap, an alphanumeric form (letters and numerals only — punctuation, brackets, and spaces stripped) with subsequence fallback for reordered text/digits (e.g. track **`161白A`** matches video **`YYY - 白161A [YYY161]`**), and **track position** when the video title includes a side suffix (e.g. duplicate track titles **`金344`** on A/B with video **`YYY - 金344 B`** matching position **B** only) or a **leading position token** on the song title (e.g. **`Untitled`** at **A1** matches **`Artist - A1 Unknown (UNK10)`** — sides **A–Z** for box sets; catalog parentheticals ignored for the generic-title check). When a video title omits mix/version suffixes that appear only on the track row (e.g. track **`Ama 76 (Original Mix)`** vs video **`Artist - Ama 76 [CATNO]`**), matching also compares **core titles** with **`()` / `[]` annotations stripped**; **remix** hints (**`Remix`**, **`Remixx`**, … — enforced on every match path, including substring overlap) and parenthetical version tokens (**`instrumental`**, **`vocal`**, …) must agree so a remix row does not steal a non-remix upload (and vice versa). Position-prefix pairing only applies when both the track title and the video song title after the position are **generic** (**`Untitled`**, **`Unknown`**, …), so a named track does not steal an **`A1 Unknown`** video by position alone. Shared title tokens after stripping artist prefixes from video titles also score. When title matching leaves equal counts of **generic** track titles (**`Untitled`**, **`Untitled01`**, **`Unknown`**, **`Track N`**, …) and leftover videos, **`buildReleasePlaybackMatchIndex`** can pair them 1:1 by tracklist order vs video **`Track N`** numbering, leading position tokens, or video order when every pair’s durations agree within **30s**. **`buildReleasePlaybackMatchIndex`** runs that matrix once per release (embeddable videos, per-track matches, preview leftovers); **`useReleaseModalPlayback`** and **`ReleasePlaybackProvider`** both memoize it so row playability, preview lists, and dock **`activeVideo`** resolution stay aligned. **`getReleasePreviewVideos`** lists embeddable videos not matched to any track. **Add to queue** (list-plus icon on a track row) calls **`addToQueue`** (deduped by **`instanceId` + `trackPosition`** — the same track may appear again in **up next** while it is still playing). When **up next** repeats the **currently playing** track or preview upload, **`playNext`** / ended advance **replays** from the start ([`playbackQueueReplay.ts`](../../src/utils/playbackQueueReplay.ts) + **`forceReload`** embed sync) instead of the metadata-only same-upload shortcut used for the next **different** row on one full-album upload. Prev/next walk the full **`queue`** (cross-release); when a video ends, the provider auto-advances via [`youtubeIframeEvents.ts`](../../src/utils/youtubeIframeEvents.ts). Queue helpers live in [`src/utils/playbackQueue.ts`](../../src/utils/playbackQueue.ts) (**`getQueueItemKey`** for dedupe and track stats; **`getQueueItemListId(item, index)`** for drawer list / `@dnd-kit` identity when the same **`instanceId:trackPosition`** appears more than once in **up next**).

Crate, notes, and card filter pill clicks do **not** open the modal. Discogs links live on title, artist, label text, and **View on Discogs** in **`fmdReleaseCardActionsBar`**. On **`/collection`**, **cover art** or **Release details** in the actions bar opens the in-app modal; **title** still opens Discogs.
